    Face-aware

    In ISL, raised eyebrows mark a question and a head shake marks a negation. Most systems drop the face. FaST-ISL adds it through a learned gate that opens only when the face carries meaning.

    Speed-robust

    People sign at different speeds. The model is trained on clips replayed between 0.5× and 2× speed and sees motion at several time scales at once.

    Streaming

    No buttons between sentences: a motion-energy pause detector finds sentence ends in live signing, so each sentence is translated as soon as you finish it.
